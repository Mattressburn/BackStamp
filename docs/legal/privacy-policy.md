# Backstamp Privacy Policy

**Effective date: [set at launch]**

We do not want your data. We want Pyrex knowledge and photos.

Everything below is what that sentence means mechanically.

## What your account is

When you sign in with Apple or Google, we store exactly one thing: the opaque
subject identifier your sign-in provider assigns. That string is the whole
account record. We do not store your email address, your name, or a profile.
We could not email you if we wanted to, because we never learn your address.

## Scan photos

When you photograph a dish to identify it, the photo goes to two places: our
server, and Google's Gemini API, the third-party AI service that performs the
identification. We use the paid tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms
for that tier, Google does not use your photos to train or improve its models,
and keeps short-term logs only to detect abuse of its own service. Google's
terms are at ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms.

Before any photo is saved on our server, we strip its embedded metadata,
including GPS location. Your location never reaches our disk, so we cannot
leak what we do not hold.

A scan photo is kept for improving identification only if you turn on the
"Help improve identification" toggle in Settings. It is off by default. With
it off, scan photos are used to identify your dish and are not kept for
training.

## Photos you share

Sharing a photo to the community catalog is a separate choice you make per
photo. A shared photo carries the attribution you picked: a display name you
chose yourself, or anonymous. Every shared photo is reviewed by a person
before it appears publicly.

## Your collection

Your collection syncs to our server so it survives a lost phone. What syncs is
item identifiers and counts: which pieces you have or want, and how many.
Your condition grades and notes never leave your device. We cannot read them.

## Prices

Price context comes from marketplace listing data, currently eBay. A price
query carries the item's name and nothing about you. eBay learns that someone
asked about a Butterprint 444 bowl, not who asked.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion lives in Settings, inside the app. Deleting your account
removes the account record, your collection, your scans, and your stored
photos, including photos you shared to the community catalog. We also revoke
your sign-in token with your provider. This is the commitment the app is
built to honor: when you leave, your data leaves with you.

## What we never do

No ads. We do not sell your data or give it away. We do not track you across
other apps or websites. Because we do not track, we do not respond to Do Not
Track browser signals; there is nothing to turn off.

## Children

Backstamp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from a child under 13. If we learn we have, we
will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app
before the change takes effect and update the effective date above.
