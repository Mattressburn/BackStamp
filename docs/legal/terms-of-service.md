# Backstamp Terms of Service

**Effective date: [set at launch]**

Backstamp is a vintage Pyrex identification and collection tracker. These are
the terms for using it, in plain words.

## Identification can be wrong

Identification is produced by an AI model reading your photo. It is often
right and sometimes wrong, and a wrong call on a rare pattern is an expensive
mistake. Verify an identification yourself, against the backstamp and a
trusted reference, before you buy, sell, or insure a piece based on it.

## Prices are context, not appraisals

Price figures in the app are market observations from named sources,
currently eBay listing data. Every figure carries its source label because
sold prices and asking prices are different claims. None of them is an
appraisal, an offer, or a promise of what your piece is worth.

Backstamp. Non-exclusive means the photo stays yours and you can do anything
you like with it elsewhere. The license ends when you delete the photo or
your account, and deletion removes the photo from the catalog.

By sharing a photo, you affirm that you took it and it is yours to share.
