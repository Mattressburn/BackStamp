# Backstamp source survey, 2026-08-12

Read-only web assessment. Nothing in the repository was modified.

## 1. PyrexLove assessment

### What they document

Two layers, and they are very different in value:

- **Pattern guide pages** (`/in/vintage-color-patterns/<name>/`): thin. Pattern name,
  alternative names, colorway prose, a one-line description, eBay search links. The
  Americana page carries no production years and no model numbers. Sub-pages exist for
  only some patterns. This layer adds little beyond CMoG.
- **Glassware piece database** (`/<slug>/glassware/`): the real asset. Each entry is a
  piece-level record, blog-styled but with recurring structured fields the entry itself
  labels "Item Type", "Years Manufactured", "Sizes and ID#s". A record carries pattern
  x model number x year x capacity x promotional status x included accessories.
  Concrete examples read during assessment or surfaced in their index:
  - Zodiac promotional Cinderella round casserole **#475**, "Holidays 1961", 2.5 qt,
    with Deluxe Candle Warmer (brass ring tray, three black plastic legs), gold
    compass-rose lid noted as often worn.
  - Spices promotional Cinderella oval casserole **#043** with wooden trivet, 1970s.
  - Black Tulip promotional decorator casserole **#043** with cradle.
  - Blue Barcode promotional covered casseroles, **470/480 series**, 1966.
  - Pink Daisy oval casseroles **#043/#045**; Friendship oval casseroles **#043/#045**;
    Butterprint turquoise round casserole **#480**.

  This is exactly the pattern-to-model-number layer CMoG lacks, and it includes the
  accessory completeness facts (cradles, warmers, trivets) that decide whether a
  promotional piece is "complete", which is where grail value lives.

### Coverage

The pattern guide index lists **200+ patterns** (vs our 184), alphabetical, with symbol
markers for the categories we are weakest in: `#` promotional, `*` unknown, `&`
advertising/customized, `%` restaurant ware/tableware. Promotional names visible on the
index alone: Balloons, Black Tulip, Bluebird, Golden Grapes, Holiday, Starburst,
Zodiac, Hex Signs. A "By Type" browse gives rough piece-database scale: about 100
casserole entries, 34 bowl entries, more per other types, so low hundreds of piece
records. Advertising/customized and restaurant-ware categories are populations CMoG
does not carry at all.

### Permission shape

- Footer: "© 2005-2026 Pyrex Love | All Rights Reserved". No license, no database
  claim, no terms-of-use page beyond a privacy policy link. Site explicitly not
  affiliated with Corning or World Kitchen.
- Run by **Linda and Bryan** (About page, signed "~ Linda and Bryan"), since 2005.
  Data gathered from their own collection, eBay, flea markets, and reference books;
  they state no pictures or text were copied from those books and that every photo is
  their own, Creative Commons, or used with explicit permission. People this careful
  about other people's copyright tend to respond well to being asked properly.
- **Contact routes:** the site's contact form is down for maintenance. Working routes:
  the Pyrex Love Flickr group (flickr.com/groups/pyrexlove/), retrying the contact
  form later, or a lead via CMoG's Pyrex Potluck feature page on the site
  (pyrex.cmog.org/content/pyrex-love). No public email address surfaced in search.

### Verdict

Restating their facts would add: (a) the item axis, pattern x model number
combinations with capacities, which we currently source from general knowledge with
nothing behind it; (b) promotional release facts at season granularity ("Holidays
1961"); (c) original accessory lists per promo piece; (d) roughly 16+ patterns beyond
our 184, concentrated in promotional/advertising/restaurant categories. Cost: the data
is embedded in prose, so harvesting means page-by-page restatement, a few hundred
pages, same discipline as the CMoG pass.

Access records. Permission-required (or courtesy-owed): PyrexLove, The Pyrex
Collector, Pyrex Passion. Corning Archives is access-gated rather than
license-gated for facts.

## 3. Ranking and recommendation

By (value beyond current holdings) x (legal ease):

1. **PyrexLove**, high value, ask-first but facts are legally free and the CMoG
   playbook is proven
2. **The Pyrex Collector**, high structure, same permission posture, slightly less
   accessory/grail detail
3. **Chronicling America + Internet Archive**, perfect legal position, high labor,
   pre-1964 only
4. **Pyrex Passion books**, deepest grail coverage, buy-and-restate plus courtesy
   outreach
5. **Corning Archives/Rakow**, ground truth, slowest
6. Everything else, marginal

**Pursue PyrexLove first.** It is the only source whose existing records directly fill
our two named gaps at once, the pattern x model number item axis and promotional
completeness facts, and the outreach costs one email using the exact approach that
already worked with CMoG. The Pyrex Collector is the natural second, and its timeline
can cross-check whatever PyrexLove yields.

## 7. Permission-shape rulings (recorded 2026-08-12, evening)

Checked after the contact hunt, before any harvest. These are the judgment calls
the growth plan requires, made with the user in session.

- **PyrexLove: outreach-gated.** robots.txt disallows ClaudeBot and Claude-Web by
  name (and ia_archiver, which is why Wayback has nothing). Facts remain
  uncopyrightable, but a bulk automated harvest against a named opt-out is against
  the owners' stated wishes. No harvest until Linda and Bryan say yes. Routes:
  Facebook Messenger (facebook.com/pyrexlove), Instagram @pyrexlove (verify
  ownership), contact-form retry, or a forwarded introduction through CMoG.
- **The Pyrex Collector: ask-first.** No robots.txt, but legal.php browsewrap
  terms require prior written permission to republish Materials, one personal
  noncommercial copy allowed. Facts are legally free; the polite read of this
  fence is the same email before any bulk restatement. Their forum registration
  is a contact route.
- **Chronicling America + Internet Archive: open.** Public domain or no known
  restrictions. Period advertisements are primary evidence for promotional
  pieces: name, model number, season, retail price. The `period-ad` provenance
  value exists for exactly this. Mining may proceed without asking anyone.
- **Pyrex Passion books: buy and restate.** A lawfully purchased reference
  book's facts are the classic Feist scenario. Courtesy note to the author when
  used.
